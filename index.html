Breast Cancer Diagnosis Using a CA15-3 Photonic Crystal Fiber–Based LSPR Sensor

Designed an anisotropic dual-core, double-side-polished photonic crystal fiber LSPR biosensor for detecting the CA15-3 breast-cancer biomarker.
