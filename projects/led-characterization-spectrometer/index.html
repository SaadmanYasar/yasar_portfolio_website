Project 14 · Optical instrumentation

Compact Camera-Based Spectrometer

Designed a compact spectrometer for characterizing visible light sources. Incoming light passes through a narrow slit, is separated into its component wavelengths, and is captured by a camera. Software calibration maps the image to wavelength so spectral peaks and relative intensity can be measured.
