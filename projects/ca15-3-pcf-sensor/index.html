Project 06 · Optical biosensing

Breast Cancer Diagnosis Using a CA15-3 Photonic Crystal Fiber–Based LSPR Sensor

Designed an anisotropic dual-core, double-side-polished photonic crystal fiber LSPR biosensor for detecting the CA15-3 breast-cancer biomarker. Full-vector FEM simulations across 1.33–1.36 RIU achieved wavelength sensitivity up to 42,000 nm/RIU and amplitude sensitivity of 105 RIU⁻¹, supporting a portable point-of-care sensing concept.
