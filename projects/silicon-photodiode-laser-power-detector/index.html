Project 15 · Optoelectronic sensing

Silicon Photodiode Laser Power Detector

Designed a dual-silicon-photodiode detector for laser-power monitoring and optical alignment. A light-blocking hood and 620–750 nm filter suppress ambient light, while integrated IR LEDs support pointing, acquisition, and tracking.
